# Public Content Release Checklist (Internal)

**Audience:** Fugluck product, engineering and legal reviewers. Keep this checklist internal; do not render it as public website copy.

**Status:** Open inputs for review drafts. No item below has been completed by this checklist.

Public pages use neutral publication-pending language where facts are not yet verified. Do not replace that language with guesses or expose bracketed editor instructions. The Legal and Policy Index identifies the categories still under review without publishing this internal action register.

## Operator and contact information

- [ ] Confirm the operating legal entity name and entity type.
- [ ] Confirm the entity registration or identification number, if applicable.
- [ ] Confirm the jurisdiction of establishment and registered office/address.
- [ ] Confirm the authorized representative or contact person, if applicable.
- [ ] Supply verified public support, legal/regulatory and privacy contact channels.

## Terms and eligibility

- [ ] Confirm minimum age and account eligibility requirements.
- [ ] Confirm supported and restricted territories.
- [ ] Approve governing-law, jurisdiction and dispute-resolution wording for the actual operator.
- [ ] Approve account suspension, appeals, multiple-account, complaints and support-handling language against enabled product behavior.
- [ ] Approve final Terms version and effective date; verify the product policy-acceptance version matches.

## Privacy and data handling

- [ ] Verify the data actually processed by the deployed product, including cookies, analytics, technical/security logs and support workflows.
- [ ] Verify the active hosting and data-processing provider list and any cross-border disclosures that are applicable.
- [ ] Set retention periods or criteria for account, competition, authority, audit, support and security records; do not invent fixed periods.
- [ ] Confirm applicable user-rights wording and a working privacy request/contact process.
- [ ] Determine whether non-essential analytics/cookies are enabled and whether consent controls are required.
- [ ] Approve final Privacy version and effective date; verify the product policy-acceptance version matches.

## Review, translations and external process

- [ ] Complete legal review of the English Terms, Privacy Policy, Competition Rules, Fair Competition and Sandbox Notice.
- [ ] Obtain qualified Georgian and Russian review of long-form legal translations before marking either language complete.
- [ ] Confirm whether a Revenue Service filing or classification process applies; identify the authoritative process, recipient, required materials, format and submission channel before making any filing claim.
- [ ] Record any bank-requested language or evidence only after the relevant classification process and actual request are verified.

## Release verification

- [ ] Check every public page against the current source copy and product behavior.
- [ ] Confirm that unresolved values remain neutral publication-pending statements in public pages.
- [ ] Confirm public Terms and Privacy remain visibly marked as drafts until reviewed and approved.
- [ ] Confirm no invented company, contact, address, legal, regulatory, retention or provider detail was added.
